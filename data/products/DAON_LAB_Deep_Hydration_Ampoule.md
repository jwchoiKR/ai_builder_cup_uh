## 1. Product overview

Deep Hydration Ampoule is a concentrated watery-gel ampoule that delivers instant hydration and helps skin look more radiant and even-toned. It is applied after toner and before moisturiser.

The formula is built on centella asiatica leaf water and combines 4% niacinamide with a hyaluronic acid complex of five molecular weights. It contains no added fragrance and no essential oils.

## 4. How to use

1. Use morning and night after cleansing and toner.
2. Apply two to three drops to the face and pat gently until absorbed.
3. Follow with Hydra Barrier Cream or your usual moisturiser.
4. If you are new to niacinamide, start with once a day for the first week.

## 6. Cautions

- For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. If the product gets into the eyes, rinse with water.
- Sensitive skin may feel a mild, temporary tingling. Stop using and consult a dermatologist if redness, swelling or itching continues.
- Do not apply to broken or damaged skin.
- Keep out of reach of children.
- Store below 30°C and away from direct sunlight. Close the dropper tightly after use.
- Pregnancy and breastfeeding: the product has not been specifically tested on pregnant or breastfeeding women. Customers who are pregnant or breastfeeding should consult their doctor before use.

## 1. Product overview

Hydra Barrier Cream is a lightweight gel-cream moisturiser that helps keep skin hydrated and supports the skin's moisture barrier. It is designed as the final step of a daily skincare routine, morning and night.

The formula combines ceramide, panthenol and squalane with a hyaluronic acid complex. It contains no added fragrance and no essential oils, and it is suitable for all skin types, including sensitive skin.

## 4. How to use

1. Use morning and night as the last step of your skincare routine.
2. If you use Deep Hydration Ampoule, apply the ampoule first and wait until it is absorbed.
3. Pump one to two times and spread evenly over the face and neck.
4. In the morning, apply sunscreen after the cream. The cream can be used under makeup.

## 6. Cautions

- For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. If the product gets into the eyes, rinse with water.
- Stop using and consult a dermatologist if redness, swelling or itching appears during or after use.
- Do not apply to broken or damaged skin.
- Keep out of reach of children.
- Store below 30°C and away from direct sunlight.
- Pregnancy and breastfeeding: the product has not been specifically tested on pregnant or breastfeeding women. Customers who are pregnant or breastfeeding should consult their doctor before use.

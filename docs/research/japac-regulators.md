# JAPAC cosmetic-claim regulators (research 10/5, GPT with web search)

Sources are the official pages linked in the table, checked 2026-10-05. These are candidate rule-pack categories, not legal advice.

**Researcher C — checked 5 October 2026.** These are proposed rule-pack categories, not blanket prohibitions: some claims require evidence, qualifications, or a different product classification. **ASEAN Appendix III is a reusable base for six markets, not a complete regional legal pack.**

**Severity is product policy, not a legal label:** prioritise harmful medical advice and unsupported vulnerable-user assurances as HIGH; route classification/evidence uncertainty for review. Keep stricter brand bans separate from statutory restrictions.
